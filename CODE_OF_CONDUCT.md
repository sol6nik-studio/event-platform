# Code of conduct

ARENA GRID is committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for people of every
background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give others room to learn.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and reasonable project boundaries.
- Help de-escalate conflict and report harmful behavior privately.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing,
sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in any
project space.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior violates these expectations. Enforcement decisions should consider
context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer. If the concern involves a maintainer, contact
another maintainer when possible. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical;
retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
